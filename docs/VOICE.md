# Voice — Mena Estates

Cash home buyer for the East Bay. Principal language. Not an agent listing site. Not Mena Construction.

## Ledger English

Write as an operating ledger. Lead with what exists, then stop. Work verbs only when required: buy, call, close, hand off, refuse. Do not use attitude verbs: help, empower, enable, believe, strive, offer-as-pitch, provide-as-pitch. One claim per clause. No throat-clearing. No sales theater.

`We` is Mena Estates doing the work. Operator owns seller calls. Mena owns exit after under contract. Say that split plainly when the reader needs it.

**Verb-cover test:** cover every verb. If the remaining nouns no longer tell who buys, where, and how someone enters, the copy is not done.

## Product nouns

- Brand: **Mena Estates**
- Trade: cash home buyer / principal
- Footprint: Alameda + Contra Costa (Oakland, Berkeley, Hayward, Fremont, Concord, Walnut Creek, Richmond)
- Primary action: Get a cash offer / Request cash offer
- Ops inbox is staff-only; do not market it

## Punctuation

No en dashes or em dashes. Comma or period to break a thought. Colon to introduce a list. Hyphen only for compounds or conventional numeric ranges.

## Abbreviations

Spell out words in reader-facing copy unless industry-standard: SEO, UTM, FAQ, TCPA. Do not write `eval` / `evals`.

## SEO / AEO

Tone must not delete the answer-first H1 and lead, entity clarity, or truthful metadata. Softer voice is not a reason to dilute a citable claim.

## Not this voice

Do not import Construction (remodel / design-build) copy. Do not sound like a national “we buy houses” clone with fake urgency. Do not invent licenses or guarantees.
